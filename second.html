Dear Dad, Today we celebrate more than the day you were born—
we celebrate the man who taught me what strength looks like when it is gentle, what love feels like when it is steady, and what wisdom sounds like when it speaks softly. You have been my quiet hero, standing firm when life tried to shake us, carrying burdens I didn’t always see, so my steps could feel lighter. In your actions, I learned integrity. In your patience, I learned grace. In your sacrifices, I learned what true love means— love that gives, protects, and believes, even without being asked. Thank you for every lesson you taught without words, for every prayer spoken and unspoken, for every time you chose us, day after day. As you add another year today, may joy find you easily, may peace sit comfortably in your heart, and may you feel—deeply and truly— how grateful I am to be your child. Happy Birthday, Dad. We honor you, We appreciate you, and We love you more than these words can ever say.
